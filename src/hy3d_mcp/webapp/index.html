Concept

One object, plain background, no drop shadow. The background gets keyed out and the subject cropped square before it reaches the generator.

Front is required; the back view is the one that pays. Same subject, same scale, turned 90° at a time — a turntable render or an orthographic sheet. Views that disagree about proportion give a worse mesh than the front alone. Cutting a contact sheet into strips is the exception worth knowing: each strip is then a fraction of the sheet's width, and the generator resizes whatever it gets to 518px — so a figure that was 200px tall in the strip arrives with a face a handful of pixels across, and no setting downstream can put back detail the conditioner never saw.
